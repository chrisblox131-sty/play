🏛️ Science & Survival Codex

Practical physical and chemical principles discovered while exploring, mining, smelting, and harvesting in the game world.

1. Mineral Durability & Rock Mass

Rocks fracture according to physical mass: Small Rocks break in 2 strikes (1–3 stones), Medium Rocks in 4 strikes (3–6 stones), and Big Monoliths in 6 strikes (7–10 stones).

Small: 2 hits Medium: 4 hits Big: 6 hits

2. Metallurgy & Thermodynamic Smelting

Refining raw iron ore is an endothermic chemical reaction. Providing coal fuel and a gold fee to the village blacksmith runs a 6-second thermal state machine with chimney smoke and anvil clangs before producing refined iron ingots.

6.0s Endothermic Coal Carbon Fuel Refined Ingot

3. Botany & Cellulose Plant Structure

Forest trees are mortal biological structures with 4 HP. Striking with an axe triggers physical vibration, depletes visual health pips, and fells timber, unlocking cellulose biopolymers.

4 Tree HP Cellulose Biopolymer Timber Felling

4. 3x3 Field Workbench Tool Multipliers

Crafting specialized survival tools on the 3x3 grid grants physical multipliers: Pickaxes double rock damage, Axes double tree chopping speed, and Swords double combat efficiency.

3x3 Grid 2x Mining 2x Chopping
