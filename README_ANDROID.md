# 📱 Android Phone & Tablet Release Guide
**The Awaiting Stars — WICE 2026 Malaysia Competition**

Play the complete native game on Android phones and tablets using either method below:

---

## ⚡ Method 1: Sideload & Install the Standalone Android App (`.apk`)
1. Copy **`pygbag_game.apk`** (13.9 MB) onto your Android phone or tablet (via USB, Google Drive, WhatsApp, or Bluetooth).
2. Tap on **`pygbag_game.apk`** in your Android file manager.
3. If prompted, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for your file manager.
4. Tap **Install**.
5. Launch **The Awaiting Stars** directly from your app drawer!
   - Runs full offline WebAssembly Python engine at 60 FPS.
   - Includes virtual analog joystick and custom arcade touch controls.

---

## 🌐 Method 2: Instant Play via Mobile Web & QR Code (Zero Install!)
1. Open your Android phone camera or QR scanner.
2. Scan **`WICE_2026_MOBILE_QR_CARD.png`** or **`mobile_qr_code.png`**.
3. In Chrome, the game opens immediately in your mobile browser!
4. *(Optional)* Tap the Chrome menu (`⋮`) and select **"Add to Home Screen"** to install as a fullscreen borderless app without browser tabs.

---

## 🎮 Mobile Touch Controls:
- **Analog Joystick (Bottom Left)**: 360° fluid character movement and walking/running.
- **[ATK] (Red)**: Primary sword slash.
- **[JUMP] (Green)**: Jump over obstacles and onto platforms.
- **[SKILL] (Blue)**: Cast Glacial Strike projectile (freezes targets for 3.5 seconds).
- **[GUARD] (Orange)**: Shield barrier against incoming projectiles and strikes.
- **[BAG] (Purple)**: Open satchel inventory and collected quest items.
- **[MENU] (Grey)**: Pause or return to Main Menu.
